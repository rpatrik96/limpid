# Coach a passage

Limpid scores your prose against *good* writing — Orwell, Strunk & White, Hemingway, the Economist, Pinker — and tells you **why** a passage fails, not just where. It names the failure pattern (Buried Lede, Idea Soup, Hedge Stacking…), gives the cognitive reason, and shows a before/after.

- Select a paragraph in a `.tex` or `.md` file (or select nothing to coach the whole file).
- Run **Limpid: Coach this selection / section**, press **⌘⌥L / Ctrl+Alt+L**, or right-click → _Coach_.
- Each card has **reveal** (jump to the passage) and, where there's a concrete rewrite, **apply fix**.

It runs locally — your drafts never leave your machine.
