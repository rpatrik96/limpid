# Learn the patterns

Open the **Limpid** icon in the Activity Bar. It has two views:

- **Coach** — the per-run report: highlighted prose, coach cards, the grade and its four dimensions (Accessibility / Clarity / Flow / Precision), and an audience-altitude control.
- **Learn** — a browsable library of the named failure patterns (definition, how to spot it, why it fails, before/after), plus a *your writing* panel: your most recurring patterns, your grade and per-dimension trend, and metric averages across runs.

The goal isn't the grade — it's pattern recognition that transfers to the next draft.
