have been proposed over the years, and several benchmarks were established by
earlier authors, and the field has, generally speaking, continued to grow in a
fairly steady and largely uninterrupted fashion for quite some time now. It
could be argued that representation learning matters, and arguably the
community has not yet reached consensus on what a good representation actually
is. Experiments were performed by the authors on a standard suite, and results
were collected and were subsequently analyzed. We use SGD throughout, and the
learning rate was tuned by us.
