Coach

flowBuried Lede

The paper's claim lands last; the opening spends two sentences on generic background.

Why
A reviewer decides what your paper is about in the first sentence. Background that any reader could write tells them nothing — the identifiability result is the news, so it should lead.
Fix
Open on the contribution, then situate it against prior work.
A great deal of prior work has explored the topic… We show that a simple identifiability criterion predicts…→We give a simple identifiability criterion that predicts when a representation recovers the latent factors — sharpening a question prior work left open.
clarityengine.passive-voice

Passive voice hides who acted.

Why
“were performed by the authors” buries the agent and adds words. Name the actor: you ran the experiments.
Fix
Rewrite in the active voice.
Experiments were performed by the authors on a standard suite.→We ran the experiments on a standard suite.
accessibilityengine.undefined-acronym

Acronym “SGD” is used before it is defined.

Why
An undefined acronym is a small cliff for any reader outside the immediate subfield — spell it out on first use.
Fix
Expand on first use: “stochastic gradient descent (SGD)”.

Why

Fix
Replace with the specific prior approaches you build on.
